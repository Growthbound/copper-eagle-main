# Copper Eagle brand voice

**Read `docs/brand/voice.md` before writing.** That file is the whole guide and
the single source of truth; this skill exists so it gets loaded at the right
moment.

The voice is drafted from the live site's own copy and is waiting on the
owner's confirmation. Write the way that file describes (friendly, local,
confident, plain), and make no claim the owner has not confirmed or published.
Local facts on city pages need a cited public source. Do not borrow wording,
offers, or claims from other sites built on this platform.
