# Copper Eagle brand voice

**Status: not written yet.** Copper Eagle's voice, vocabulary, and claims have
not been defined. Each section below says what it will hold. Until the owner
supplies them, follow the interim rules in each section and nothing more.

This file is binding on every word a customer reads: website and service pages,
blog articles, FAQs, emails, texts, ads, calls to action, page titles, and meta
descriptions. It is silent on code, comments, commit messages, and internal
documentation.

## Core brand personality

To be defined with the owner: who Copper Eagle is, who it serves, and how it
should come across.

Interim rule: sound like a capable local business talking to a customer. Plain,
calm, and direct. No personality traits the owner has not described.

## Primary voice principles

To be defined with the owner.

Interim rules:

- Put the useful answer first.
- Prefer specific actions to adjectives. Say what happens, not how great it is.
- Use short sentences and everyday words.
- Write to one reader, in the second person.

## Words and phrases to avoid

To be defined with the owner.

Interim rule: avoid hype ("best in town", "world-class"), fear-based selling,
fake urgency ("act now", "limited time"), and canned marketing filler.

## Accuracy and integrity

Never publish a claim the owner has not confirmed: founding year, licenses,
certifications, insurance, awards, reviews or ratings, prices, guarantees,
service areas, response times, staff names, or project results. Missing
evidence blocks the claim. It is not permission for a placeholder.

Legal and regulated pages (privacy policy, terms and conditions, cookie policy,
disclaimers) come from the owner or a legal review and are never rewritten as
marketing copy.

## Review checklist

Before any customer-facing copy ships:

- [ ] Every factual claim is confirmed by the owner.
- [ ] Nothing is borrowed from another brand built on this platform.
- [ ] The useful answer comes first.
- [ ] No hype, fear, or fake urgency.
- [ ] Phone, email, and address come from site settings, not the copy.
- [ ] Legal text is untouched unless a rewrite was explicitly authorized.
